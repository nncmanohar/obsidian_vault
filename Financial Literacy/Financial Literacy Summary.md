
The goal of pursuing **financial literacy** is to achieve **financial freedom**.

**Financial Freedom / Independence:**

You are financially independent when you can maintain your current lifestyle without having to depend on your current active employment for income.

To achieve this, it is important to have **adequate insurance (risk mitigation), emergency funds (risk mitigation), income greater than expenses , more assets than liabilities (with some exceptions), multiple sources of income, and eventually, sufficient passive income**.

It is important to understand how money works. **Learn to earn, learn to invest, and let your money work for you.**

**Earn → Save → Invest → Grow → Generate passive income → Achieve financial freedom.**

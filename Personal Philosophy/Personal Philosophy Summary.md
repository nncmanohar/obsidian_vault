Understanding Happiness:

-----------

**Be in the moment**

**Decades, years, and days slip away, but the only place you can ever make an impact is right now.** Even planning for the future or reflecting on the past must happen in the present. **Be in the moment**, because life only happens in the current second.

-------
**Absolute wealth matters less than the direction of progress


**Two brothers each have ₹2 crores, but one is miserable and the other is ecstatic.** 
The first brother started with ₹3 crores and lost ₹1 crore, while the second started with ₹1 crore and gained ₹1 crore. 

This proves that **absolute wealth matters less than the direction of progress.** Happiness is determined by momentum, not the current baseline.

------------
**Every habit serves a purpose; to replace a bad habit, substitute it while keeping the same core benefit.**

- **The Trigger:** Understand the underlying emotion or social need driving the habit.
- **The Trap:** Smoking or drinking may start as a way to bond, but they quickly become destructive. Beware of milder traps like late-night dinners or excessive tea breaks.
- **The Substitute:** Trade destructive habits for constructive ones that satisfy the same social or emotional need (e.g., run clubs, book clubs, finance classes, or work collaborations).

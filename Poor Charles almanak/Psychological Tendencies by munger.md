# Reward and Punishment super reaction tendency


Rewards = Incentives
Punishments = Disincentives

Even a great thinker like Charlie Munger gets surprises from the power of this tendency.

## Federal Express Case
Goal : 
	Get all the packages shifted rapidly among airplanes in central airport each night.

Non-Aligned incentive : 
	Hourly billing. Goal is not to maximize hourly billing
Aligned incentive : 
	Make it a shift and every one go home once all the planes are loaded.


## Xerox Case

Scenario : New machines were selling poorly compared to in relation to its older and inferior machine.

Commission arrangements provided a Perversive incentive to sell the old machines.

## Appeal to interest not to reason
If you would persuade, appeal to interest and not to reason.

Always think about incentives first.

Get the incentives right - the most import rule in management.


>[!IMPORTANT]
> Repeat behavior that works

Don't reward employees for the behavior  you don't want more of.

Prompt reward works much better than the delayed reward in changing and maintaining behavior.

The withdrawal pattern of reward that keeps the reflexive behavior longest is : Random Distribution. -> Example : Gambling Compulsion.

Avoid Man with a hammer tendency

**Incentive Caused Bias**

Incentives can make a man **drift into immoral behavior** in order to get what he wants by rationalizing his behavior.

Example: A doctor who performed many surgeries and believed gallbladder is source of all medical vial and if you really loved your patient, you could not get that organ out rapidly enough.

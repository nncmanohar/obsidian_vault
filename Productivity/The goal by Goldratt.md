

The goal by Goldratt, this is one of the best book I have ever read on understanding the agile. Here are the main lessons

The output of the system is decided by the bottleneck. 

Goldratt's **Theory of Constraints** : 
The constraint is something you **identify through the behavior of the system**, then exploit, subordinate the rest of the system to it, elevate it, and repeat as the constraint moves.



The goal of a company is to **make money (profits)**, but making money in isolation is not enough. The business must generate an adequate **return on the capital invested**.

At the same time, it must maintain sufficient **liquidity and cash flow** to remain financially healthy and meet its obligations.

A strong business also **cycles its capital quickly**: the faster it can turn invested resources into sales and cash, and reinvest them, the more effectively the system can grow. **Walmart is a classic example of this principle**, with its high inventory turnover and rapid conversion of inventory into cash.
